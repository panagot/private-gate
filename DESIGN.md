# Private Gate design

## Scene

Customs desk at dusk. Brass stamp, ink pad, paper forms. Credential clearance — not a neon vault.

## Strategy

Deep charcoal ground, warm brass accent, chalk paper panels. Typography: Fraunces (display) + IBM Plex Sans (UI) + IBM Plex Mono (ids).

## Motion

Hero stamp rise, gate underline, live pulse on claim success. Transform/opacity only, ease-out-quart.

## Anti-slop

No purple gradients, no Inter, no nested card stacks, no emoji icons.
